Servings: 8 | Prep: 10–15 min | Cook: 45 min

### Ingredients

#### For the Onion Topping

* Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil, for frying
* ¼ cup cassava flour
* ¼ cup arrowroot flour
* ½ tsp salt, plus extra for seasoning
* ½ onion, sliced super thin

#### For the Casserole

* ½ onion, diced
* ½ cup mushrooms (we used shiitake), sliced thin
* 3¼ cups fresh green beans
* 1 tbsp cassava flour
* 4 tbsp Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil
* ½ tsp salt
* ½ tsp black pepper
* 2 jars Primal Kitchen Cashew Kale Pesto Alfredo Sauce
* or Primal Kitchen Creamy Cashew Alfredo Sauce (for a lighter flavor) _(wording from the page text, not the recipe card; the card lists this sauce with no quantity)_

### Directions

#### Make the Crispy Onion Topping

1. Heat the fryer to 390°F. We used an Instant Pot and fry basket to shallow fry the onions, but you can use a pot on the stove. Add enough oil to your vessel so it reaches about halfway up the sides of the pot.
2. Slice half of an onion into thin rings, and cut those rings in half.
3. Make the batter by combining both flours and salt. Toss onion slices in it to coat.
4. Sprinkle the onions into the oil, a small batch at a time. They will clump together in a nest (and that's OK).
5. Fry for 2–3 minutes and flip. Fry for another 2–3 minutes. Don't fry all at once—fry in batches, depending on the size of your fryer or pot.
6. Remove onions from oil with tongs and place on a paper towel-lined cookie sheet.
7. Quickly season with salt (lightly). Let cool, and then break the onion nests apart.

#### Make the Casserole

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Boil green beans until tender, about 4–5 minutes. Drain and set aside.
3. Heat 1 tbsp avocado oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sauté mushrooms and onions for about 8 minutes or until golden. Season with salt and pepper, and set aside.
4. In the same skillet, heat 3 tbsp avocado oil. Sprinkle in cassava flour and whisk for one minute until golden.
5. Pour alfredo sauce into the skillet and whisk for about a minute.
6. Add in the mushroom/onion mix, and whisk.
7. Add in cooked green beans, and turn off the stove.
8. Pour beans and sauce into a 9 x 13" baking dish.
9. Top with fried onions and bake, uncovered, for 30 minutes. Serve and enjoy!

### Make Ahead

* _From the page text (not the recipe card):_ "...you can make the casserole part the day before, cover it and refrigerate it, fry the onion straws, and then bake the casserole on the big day to save a bit of time."
